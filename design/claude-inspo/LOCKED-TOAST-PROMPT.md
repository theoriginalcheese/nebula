## Locked format

Every toast is the same row. Same height, same chip, same type size, same gaps, same vertical centre. A prompt is not a different component. It is the same row, only wider, so two buttons fit on it.

- Status: 384×60. Full pill, radius = 30.
- Prompt: 456×60. Full pill, radius = 30. Not taller.
- One line, vertically centred: 28px chip, then the title, a middot, then the game name.
- Prompt only: Record and Not now on that same line, at the right, same centre as the words. They do not sit underneath. They do not stack. They do not get their own band.
- Seven specks. On a status toast they cluster at the right end. On a prompt they stay by the chip, clear of the buttons.
- A 2px line along the bottom, fading at both ends. Ember on start, stop, and error. Violet on pause, resume, and prompt.
- Glass: ground #0A0812 and #100D1C, violet #8B7CF6, ember #FF5C7A, text #F5F3FF, secondary #9A93C4. Segoe UI Variable. Nebula showing through a 3px inset. No grey frame.

## Do not draw

- A taller prompt. Anything at 404×116 or with buttons under the words.
- A stacked title over the game name.
- A centred column, a split with buttons stacked on the right, or a band of buttons under a band of words.
- A squarer or rounder border than the others. Every card is the same pill.
- Specks that only twinkle in place.
- A delete action, a bitrate, or a fake number that is not the 10:47 · 1.7 GB on the stopped toast.
