Borrowed from the toast, not the console

The toast is the one Nebula surface with its own vocabulary, so the phone takes it rather than shrinking the desktop panes. Four things came across: the dust constellation (tinted specks in six named motions, transform and opacity only), the 28px chip with its 0 0 14px tinted glow, the drain track masked to transparent at both ends, and the two-layer pill.

All of this is buildable, with three caveats

The Live Activity, its transport buttons, the Time Sensitive level and the actionable prompt are ActivityKit, App Intents and UNNotificationAction respectively, all real. Three things to plan for: the phone cannot poll a sleeping PC, so state arrives by push from the agent and the Live Activity is updated with a push token; Wake over LAN needs a always-on peer on the tailnet to send the magic packet, since the phone will not be on the same L2; and App Review will want the streaming screen to be a launcher into Moonlight rather than a bundled decoder.

Motion carries state, never decoration

Every animation here is doing a job. The arc turns only while recording and stops dead when it ends, the rule the tray icon already follows. The orb quickens during a handshake. The ribbon bars stagger once on arrival and never again. Nothing loops for the sake of looking alive.

One easing, everywhere

Every transition is cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1), the --ease token straight out of the generated sheet. Presses scale to .97 rather than changing colour, so the feedback is physical and survives any accent choice.

What the design library changed

Its dark-first note argues elevation is a lightness ladder, never a shadow, and that glows are legitimate lighting on dark. So cards lift by surface step (#100D1C → #181428) plus a 1px inset highlight; the only real shadow left is under the floating tab bar, where blur has to sell that it is above the page.
