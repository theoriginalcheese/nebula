## Keyboard

- **Closed:** Space / Enter / ↑ / ↓ on trigger opens the panel (↑ starts on previous option).
- **Open:** ↑↓ move highlight, Enter selects and closes, Esc closes without changing value.
- Click outside closes without changing value; click row selects and closes.
